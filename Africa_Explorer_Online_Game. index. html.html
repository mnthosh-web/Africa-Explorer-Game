    Quick map reminder

    Africa is bordered by the Atlantic Ocean to the west and the Indian Ocean to the east and southeast. The Mediterranean Sea lies to the north and the Red Sea to the northeast. Madagascar is an island country off southeastern Africa. Zanzibar is an island group belonging to Tanzania. A landlocked country has no coastline on an ocean or sea.
